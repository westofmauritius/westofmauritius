## Footage for the homepage video

The homepage can play a silent, looping video behind "The best coast."
Everything is ready; it only needs the footage. Until then the photo shows,
exactly as now.

**What to film:** 10 to 20 seconds of the west coast that loops calmly:
waves on the lagoon at Tamarin, palms moving, the light on Le Morne. Steady
camera (a tripod or a drone hovering), no people's faces, no text, no
sound needed. Late afternoon light suits the site best. It must be your own
footage or footage you have the right to use.

**What to send:** the original file in the best quality you have (MP4 or
MOV, landscape, 1920 × 1080 or larger). Send it to Claude in a session and
ask for it to be added: it will be converted to small web files (AV1 WebM
plus an MP4 fallback, a phone sized version too) and switched on in
`src/lib/hero-video.ts`.

**Good to know:** the video only loads after the page itself, never on slow
connections, with data saver on or for visitors who prefer less motion,
and visitors can pause it with the button in the corner.
