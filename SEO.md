# SEO keyword plan

The strategy: **own the west coast**. National sites and big travel brands
cover "Mauritius" broadly; we go deep on one coast, area by area and
question by question, and we win on trust: the guide is written by a
Mauritian, every fact is sourced and dated, and nothing paid for is hidden.

How to use this file:

- One page, one main search. Before adding a page, check this table: if a
  page already targets the search, improve that page instead.
- **Priority** is a judgement of search demand and value (leads, affiliate
  potential), not measured volume. Check it against Google Search Console
  (once live) and Google Keyword Planner, and update the table.
- **Status**: live = real content, indexable. Placeholder = structure only,
  noindex until written (see TODO_OLIVIER.md).

## Angle in every title and description

- Say where: "west coast of Mauritius", the village name, "Mauritius".
- Say who: "by a Mauritian", "from a local" where it fits naturally,
  especially on living and buying pages, where trust decides the click.
- Answer the question in the description, do not tease it.

## Avoiding pages that compete with each other

- **"Living in X" lives on the area page** (`/en/areas/x#living`), not on a
  separate page. One strong page per village ranks for both "Tamarin" and
  "living in Tamarin"; two pages would split the links and confuse Google.
  So "living in Black River" is targeted by `/en/areas/black-river`.
- **Costs**: the area page's costs table gives the headline figures; the
  "cost of living in Tamarin" page goes deeper (budgets per household) and
  links back. Only Tamarin has a cost page for now; add others only where
  Search Console shows demand.
- **Buying**: area buying guides ("buying property in Tamarin as a
  foreigner") cover schemes and projects in that area only and link to the
  scheme pages (PDS, IRS …) for the rules, instead of repeating them.
- Comparisons are named in the order people search them ("Tamarin vs Grand
  Baie", not the reverse).

## Internal links (already built)

- Every area page: its Living section lists the questions, comparisons and
  buying guide for that area.
- Every living article: links to the area pages it covers ("Living in X")
  and to related articles.
- The hub links to every question, comparison, area Living section and
  buying page.
- Guides link to their areas and places; place pages link back to guides.

## Technical checklist (built in)

- Unique title and description per page; canonical on every page.
- `sitemap.xml` with `lastmod`, English only while French is hidden;
  placeholders never listed.
- Structured data: Organization and Person (author), Article with author,
  BreadcrumbList, Place for areas, places by type, FAQPage once answers
  are real.
- Placeholder pages and French pages are `noindex`.
