## In short

Soley uses **no advertising or tracking cookies**. Visitor statistics are
only counted if you allow them, and even then without cookies.

## What we store on your device

| Name                                       | Type                       | Purpose                                                    | How long                                     |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `soley_session` or Supabase `sb-…` cookies | Cookie, strictly necessary | Keeps you signed in to your account                        | Up to 30 days, or until you sign out         |
| `soley-quote-draft-v1`                     | Browser storage            | Saves the quote form as you fill it in, so nothing is lost | Until the request is sent or you start again |
| `soley-analytics-consent-v1`               | Browser storage            | Remembers your statistics choice                           | Until you change it                          |

Strictly necessary storage does not need your consent. You can clear all of
it in your browser settings.

## Statistics (only with your permission)

If you choose "Allow statistics", we load Plausible Analytics. It counts
visits without cookies and without collecting personal data. You can change
your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page.

## Bot protection

Our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to stop automated spam. It processes
technical information about your browser to tell people and bots apart.
