## What we collect and why

**When you request quotes:** your name, e-mail, phone and WhatsApp numbers,
how you prefer to be contacted, your district and town, information about
your home and roof, your electricity bill, your timing and financing
preferences, and any notes you add.
_Purpose:_ to find up to three suitable installers and send them your
request. _Legal basis:_ your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)) and steps at your
request before a contract (art. 6(1)(b)).

**Your consent:** we record the date and time, the language and the version
of the text you agreed to. _Purpose:_ to be able to prove the consent.
_Legal basis:_ legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c)).

**When an installer applies:** company details, contact person, regions,
certificates and the documents uploaded. _Purpose:_ to vet the installer
and, if approved, to send requests and invoices. _Legal basis:_ contract
(art. 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interest in vetting partners (art. 6(1)(f)).

**When you sign in:** your e-mail address and a session cookie.
_Purpose:_ to give you access to your account. _Legal basis:_ contract.

**Reviews:** your rating, text and the name you choose to show.
_Purpose:_ to help other households choose. _Legal basis:_ consent.

**Statistics:** only if you allow it, Plausible counts visits without
cookies and without collecting personal data.

**Security:** your IP address is used briefly for bot protection
(Cloudflare Turnstile) and to limit repeated form submissions.
_Legal basis:_ legitimate interest in protecting the service.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object
to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. You can withdraw
your consent at any time; this does not affect what was done before.

In your account, under **My data**, you can download your data and delete
it yourself. For anything else, contact us.

You can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or to
the Data Protection Office of Mauritius.

## Security

Data is stored encrypted in transit and at rest with our providers. Access
is limited to the people who need it, and every table is protected by access
rules in the database.
