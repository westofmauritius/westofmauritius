## 1. The service

Soley is an independent online service that passes a household's request
for rooftop solar quotes to up to three installers that Soley has vetted,
and lets the household compare the quotes. Soley is not the CEB, not a
government service and not an installer, and it does not process grant
applications.

## 2. For households

- The service is **free** for households.
- You are **never obliged** to accept a quote.
- Your contract for an installation is between you and the installer you
  choose. Soley is not a party to it and does not carry out, supervise or
  guarantee the installation.
- Vetting means that Soley checked an installer's documents and visited
  them before approving them. It is not a guarantee of the quality of any
  particular installation.
- The calculator and the comparison show **estimates** based on average
  values. Only an installer's quote gives a price.
- Information about the government grant and CEB schemes is a summary to
  help you prepare. The official pages always take precedence.
- Reviews must be honest, about your own experience, and must not contain
  personal data about others. Soley may refuse or remove reviews that break
  these rules.
