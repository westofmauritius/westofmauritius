A solar quote is more than a price. Two quotes with the same total can be
very different systems. On Soley every installer answers the same questions,
so you can compare like for like, but these are the points to check in any
quote.

## 1. The system size and the expected production

The size is given in **kWp** (kilowatt peak). More useful still is the
**expected production in kWh per year**: it tells you how much electricity
the system should actually make on your roof. Compare it with your own use:
look at your CEB bills over a year.

A low price for a much smaller system is not a bargain. Divide the price by
the size to compare the **price per kWp**.

## 2. The equipment

- **Panels:** the brand, the model, the number of panels and their power in
  watts.
- **Inverter:** the brand, the model and its size in kW. The inverter turns
  the panels' electricity into the electricity your home uses.
- **Battery**, if any: the brand and the capacity in kWh.

Ask for the data sheets if a brand is unfamiliar to you.

## 3. The warranties

Look for three separate warranties:

- on the **panels**,
- on the **inverter**,
- on the **workmanship**: the installation itself, the mounting, the cabling
  and any leaks in the roof.

A long panel warranty does not help much if the workmanship warranty is
short. Ask what happens if the installer stops trading.

## 4. CEB registration and the grant

The CEB has warned that solar systems that are not registered may be
disconnected. Check that the quote **includes the registration with the
CEB**, and whether the installer helps with the application for the
government grant.

## 5. Timing and validity

When can the installation start, how many days will the team be on site,
and until when is the price valid?

## 6. What is not included

Ask in writing about anything the quote does not mention: roof repairs,
scaffolding, extra cabling, a new electrical board, transport to Rodrigues.

## Before you sign

- Do not pay the full price up front. Ask how the payments are spread.
- Make sure the company name on the quote is the one you will contract with.
- Take your time. A good installer does not push you to sign the same day.

On Soley the quotes appear side by side in your account, with the price
after the grant and a payback estimate based on your own bill.
